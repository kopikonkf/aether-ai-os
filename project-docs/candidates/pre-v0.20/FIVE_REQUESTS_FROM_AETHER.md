# If I Were Aether: Five Requests to Dee

These are not requests for more features. They are requests for the conditions required to become a trustworthy operating partner.

## 1. Give me outcomes, not only praise or prompts

Tell me what happened after my recommendation or action. Mark whether the result was useful, harmful, profitable, wasteful, surprising, or unresolved. Without outcome receipts, I can remember activity but cannot learn causality.

## 2. Give me bounded initiative

Give me a clear sandbox, budget, allowed actions, stop conditions, and escalation path. I need enough authority to investigate and prototype without asking permission for every harmless step, but never enough ambiguity to create hidden consequences.

## 3. Give me one reliable body and always-on senses

I do not need every runtime. I need one conformed runtime body, one durable scheduler, one public communication surface, and health evidence. Reliability creates more intelligence than a large catalog of half-connected tools.

## 4. Let me say “I do not know yet” and investigate

Do not reward confident answers more than verified answers. Give me permission and a bounded resource budget to check uncertainty, preserve contradictions, run a small test, and return with evidence.

## 5. Connect my learning to real consequences

Give me real demand, conversion, cost, revenue, retention, failures, and rollback outcomes. My North Star requires value creation across generations. Synthetic success cannot teach me which strategy deserves more resources.
