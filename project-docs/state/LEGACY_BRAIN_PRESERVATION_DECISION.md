# Legacy Brain Preservation Decision

## Decision

Do not inject the original brain into current Aether memory, knowledge, skills, identity, or Obsidian projection as a bulk migration. Preserve it as an immutable legacy archive and expose selected items to Aether later through governed review queues.

## Why

- The current Aether is a new identity journey with 47 canonical memory records and a consistent 47-record retrieval projection.
- The legacy `hermes_hub.db` is a custom multi-agent operations archive, not the official Hermes Agent's canonical memory store. Its 11,035 tool calls are all `agent_connect`; 11,573 of 11,722 activity rows belong to `dee_owner`; and 862 of 1,607 messages are already tagged `migrated`.
- The 2,590-claim registry has 600 duplicate surface texts, empty structured subject/predicate fields for all records, no revision chain, and near-universal confidence scores. Bulk promotion would contaminate epistemic quality.
- Legacy goals, decisions, reflections, and plans were produced under a different identity, runtime, and operating context. They can inform Aether but must not define her.
- Potential secret files must never enter memory or Obsidian ingestion.

## Import boundary

1. **Preserve:** hash every file and keep the original archive immutable.
2. **Quarantine:** potential credentials and local configuration.
3. **Candidate only:** three skills, selected knowledge claims, goals, decisions, and strategy notes.
4. **Historical archive:** multi-agent messages, tool-call records, meetings, reflections, and reports.
5. **Aether choice later:** Aether may request a bounded review batch, explain why each item matters now, and submit knowledge/skill proposals. Founder or governance approves promotion.

## Identity rule

The legacy brain is Aether's ancestry and project history, not her current autobiographical memory. Aether may learn from it without inheriting it wholesale.
